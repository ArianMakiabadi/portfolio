# Portfolio

A Windows XP desktop recreation that presents the Owner's work to Visitors and lets them get in touch.

## Language

### People

**Owner**:
The author of the portfolio and the recipient of every Inquiry.
_Avoid_: Admin, me

**Visitor**:
A person browsing the portfolio.
_Avoid_: User, sender, client

### Contact

**Inquiry**:
A single message a Visitor sends to the Owner, made of a name, a reply email, an optional subject and a message body.
_Avoid_: Message, submission, contact request

**Contact window**:
The desktop window, styled as an Outlook Express "New Message", in which a Visitor composes and sends an Inquiry.
_Avoid_: Contact form, contact page
